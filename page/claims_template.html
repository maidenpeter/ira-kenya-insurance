How the numbers are built

IRA publishes each insurer's claims movement every quarter: claims outstanding at the start, claims reported and revived, claims paid, declined and closed as no claim, and claims still outstanding at the end. The payment ratio is IRA's own: claims paid in the quarter over the claims open to the insurer in the quarter, which is paid plus declined plus closed plus still outstanding. Over a year it is pooled, quarter by quarter.

Three businesses are shown. Liability claims are claims by third parties, most of them injury and damage from motor accidents. Non-liability claims are the insured's own: medical, motor own damage, property and the rest. Life covers death, maturity, pension and funeral claims under long-term business.

    Source: Insurance Regulatory Authority, Kenya, quarterly claims settlement statistics, Q1 2017 to Q1 2026 (summaries of insurers' unaudited monthly returns). Regulator actions: Capital FM, IRA on X. Extraction, the name crosswalk and the checks are in ira-kenya-data; this analysis is in ira-kenya-insurance. Everything here is public data.

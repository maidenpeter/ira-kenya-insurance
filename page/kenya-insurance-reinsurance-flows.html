Method

How the numbers are built

Every figure is a fourth-quarter, year-to-date figure from IRA's quarterly returns as extracted and checked in ira-kenya-data, in KSh. Gross premium is the class table's gross premium income, which is gross direct premium plus inward reinsurance. Net premium is the class table's net premium income. Premium ceded is gross less net premium, and it agrees with the revenue account's outward reinsurance for every insurer and year to within .

cession rate = (gross premium - net premium) / gross premium
retention = net premium / gross premium = 1 - cession rate

Insurers are IRA's insurer segment, micro insurers included, as the Loss Map counts the market. The local reinsurers are IRA's reinsurer segment. Motor Commercial PSV is counted with Motor Commercial.

    Source: Insurance Regulatory Authority, Kenya, quarterly industry statistics, full years (unaudited returns). Cedant grades: the Cedant Scorecard in ira-kenya-reinsurance. Everything here is public data; nothing comes from inside any insurer or reinsurer.
